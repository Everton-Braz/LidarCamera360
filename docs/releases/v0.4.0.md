## LidarCamera360 v0.4.0

Rig profiles for any setup, safer calibration, a documented project folder and cleaner exported clouds.
The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Everton-Braz/LidarCamera360/blob/v0.4.0/CHANGELOG.md).

### Rig profiles and calibration
* **Rig profiles v2**: one file describes the scanner and every camera (main 360 camera plus auxiliary cameras with
  their own clock), with model, intrinsics and mount. The Rig Calibration page is now a profile editor with a New rig
  wizard and a "Validate on a capture" action.
* **Per-capture photometric rig refinement**: time offset and mount are refined by multi-view colour consistency and
  used only when they improve held-out frames. `lidarcamera360 calibrate` runs it alone.
* **Any camera model**: THIN_PRISM_FISHEYE, OPENCV_FISHEYE, PINHOLE, OPENCV and EQUIRECTANGULAR, including phones and
  wide auxiliary cameras. Equirectangular MP4 exports take their gyro from the sibling INSV.
* **Safety gates**: implausible rigs are rejected, calibrations made for another frame size are refused, and a
  locked time offset is never moved by the SfM alignment.
* About 3x faster refinement.

### Colorization and clean clouds
* **Time-gated colorization**: each view colours only LiDAR points scanned close to its capture time.
* **`--remove-dynamic`** removes moving and standing people from exported clouds; the operator removal radius
  defaults to 1 m when person masks are used.
* **Person masks** now find passers-by too, including people who are small in the frame or seen by an upside-down
  camera. Masks from earlier versions are generated again on the next run, and masking takes longer.
* Rig profiles can carry mask settings (fisheye border, scanner body shapes).
* Motion-blurred frames are left out of colorization and the 3DGS export.

### Timelapse captures, SLAM and 3DGS
* Timelapse captures work end to end, with the rig lever arm preserved and the calibrated clock offset used for
  poses.
* SLAM loop closure survives pose jumps and rejects wrong ICP loops; `--slam-dir` imports an existing SLAM.
* Recordings that start while walking no longer give a tilted map: it is leveled to gravity.
* Images from upside-down cameras are turned upright in the 3DGS export, together with masks and poses.
* 3DGS poses are bundle-adjusted against the SfM tracks while held to the LiDAR trajectory.
* Optional RoMa v2 densification (`--densify roma`) adds dense points only on the LiDAR surface.
* LiDAR + IMAGE bags can be processed without an INSV video.

### Projects and export
* **Project folder (`lc360-project` v1)**: each output folder keeps a validated `project.json` naming its cloud,
  images, camera poses and trajectory. There is no separate project file; the folder is the project. The contract is
  documented in `spec/SPEC.md`.
* **`export`** writes PCD, LAZ/LAS, GeoJSON, GPS tracks and trajectories from a project after the run.
* Trim and chunks cut the second lens file and auxiliary videos as well.
* `--trim-end-at`, `--chunks` and `--sfm-matcher` (`sift` or `aliked-lightglue`).
* Georeferencing solves north from evidence and reports how far to trust it.

### Known limitations
* North derived from imagery can pass the quality gates and still be wrong. Check the georeferenced result before
  relying on it.
